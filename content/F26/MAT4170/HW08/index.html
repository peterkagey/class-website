Choice Exercise 7 [7] (Vector spaces over a finite field)

It turns out that many (but not all) of the theorems that you learned about vector spaces over \(\mathbb{R}\) in linear algebra also work over finite fields.

  1. Let \(\mathbb{Z}_5^{3 \times 3}\) be the ring of \(3 \times 3\) matrices over the field \(\mathbb{Z}_5\). Compute the determinant \(\det(A) \in \mathbb{Z}_5\) where \[ A = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}. \]
  2. Because the determinant of \(A\) is nonzero, the matrix is invertible. Adapt the standard matrix inversion techniques to compute the inverse matrix \(A^{-1} \in \mathbb{Z}_5^{3 \times 3}\), which should have entries that are integers mod \(5\).
  3. Compute \(\det(A^{-1})\) and show that \(\det(A^{-1}) = \det(A)^{-1}\), where the inverse is with respect to multiplication mod \(5\).

Choice Exercise 10 [≤12] (Group of units)

Let \((R, +, \times)\) be a ring. We define the group of units (under \(\times\)) as the set of elements in \(R\) that have a multiplicative inverse in \(R\): \[ R^\times = \{ r \in R \mid \exists\, r^{-1} \in R \}. \]

  1. [4] Prove that for every ring \((R, +, \times)\), \(R^\times\) forms a group under \(\times\).
  2. [1] Consider the ring \(\mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}\) of \(2 \times 2\) real valued matrices under matrix addition and matrix multiplication. Describe the group of units in words. (We call this group of units \(GL_2(\mathbb{R})\), the "general linear group.")
  3. [1] Describe the group of units for \(\mathbb{Z}\), \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\mathbb{R}\), and \(\mathbb{C}\).
  4. [1] Describe the group of units for \(\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}\), \(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}\), and \(\mathbb{Z}/15\mathbb{Z}\).
    (Hint: you did this on HW 3.)
  5. [2] Describe the group of units for the Gaussian integers \[ \mathbb{Z}[i] = \{ a + bi \mid a, b \in \mathbb{Z} \} \] and prove that the group of units is isomorphic to the cyclic group \(C_4\).
  6. [3] Describe the group of units for the endomorphism ring defined in Choice Exercise 8.
