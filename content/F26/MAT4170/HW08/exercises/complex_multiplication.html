Recall that in the ring of complex numbers \(\mathbb{C}, +, \times\), we define multiplication such that \[ (a_1 + b_1i) \times (a_2 + b_2i) = (a_1a_2 - b_1b_2) + (a_1b_2 + a_2b_1)i. \]
  1. [2] Prove that complex multiplication is associative.
  2. [1] Prove that complex multiplication is commutative.
  3. [2] Prove that complex multiplication distributes over addition. (Explain why knowing that multiplication is commutative implies that you only need to check one direction.)
  4. [2] Prove that nonzero complex numbers have multiplicative inverses by computing the multiplicative inverse of \(a + bi\).
