Let \((R, +, \times)\) be a ring. We define the group of units (under \(\times\)) as the set of elements in \(R\) that have a multiplicative inverse in \(R\): \[ R^\times = \{ r \in R \mid \exists\, r^{-1} \in R \}. \]

  1. [4] Prove that for every ring \((R, +, \times)\), \(R^\times\) forms a group under \(\times\).
  2. [1] Consider the ring \(\mathbb{R}^{2 \times 2}\) of \(2 \times 2\) real valued matrices under matrix addition and matrix multiplication. Describe the group of units in words. (We call this group of units \(GL_2(\mathbb{R})\), the "general linear group.")
  3. [1] Describe the group of units for \(\mathbb{Z}\), \(\mathbb{Q}\), \(\mathbb{R}\), and \(\mathbb{C}\).
  4. [1] Describe the group of units for \(\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z}\), \(\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}\), and \(\mathbb{Z}/15\mathbb{Z}\).
