  \item[Difficulty:]
  \item[Tools/resources/collaborators:]
  \item[Questions/Feedback:] % Fill out for up to two exercises
\end{description}
    Where you specify...
    • the number of points you attempted (e.g. 7),
    • your self-assessed score out of that number of points (e.g. 6/7),
    • approximately much time you spent on the exercise (e.g. 52 minutes),
    • your assessment of the difficulty of the exercise (e.g. 8/10),
    • any tools you used (e.g. LLMs or other AI tools), resources you used that aren't listed in the syllabus (e.g. Math StackExchange) and any collaborators you worked with on this exercise. (Remember to state what you learned from these resources.)
