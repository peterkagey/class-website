  1. [2] The quiz from Thursday of week 7 asked you about conjugacy under homomorphism.
    Answer key.

    Since \(g\) and \(g'\) are conjugate, there exists some \(\ell \in L\) such that \[ \ell \star g \star \ell^{-1} = g', \] and since \(\phi\) is structure preserving, \[ \phi(\ell \star g \star \ell^{-1}) = \phi(\ell) \bullet \phi(g) \bullet \phi(\ell^{-1}). \] We showed on Tuesday that if \(h = \phi(\ell)\), then \(h^{-1} = \phi(\ell^{-1})\), and thus \(\phi(g')\) is \(\phi(g)\) conjugated by \(h = \phi(\ell)\).
