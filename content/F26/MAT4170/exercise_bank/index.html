Exercise 2 [4] (Generated subgroups are the smallest subgroups)

Let \(G\) be a group and suppose that \(g_1, g_2, \dots, g_n \in G\). Recall that we defined \(\langle g_1, g_2, \dots, g_n\rangle\) to be the set of words consisting of letters in \(\langle g_1, g_2, \dots, g_n\rangle\). Prove that this is the smallest subgroup of \(G\) containing \(g_1, g_2, \dots, g_n\).
