Let \(G = D_4\), the dihedral group of the square.

  1. Write down all of the elements of the subgroup \[ H = \langle rf, r^2 \rangle \] and the (left) coset \[ rH = \{ r * h \mid h \in H \}, \] which "shifts" \(H\) by \(r\).
  2. Write all elements of the cosets \(r^3fH = \{ r^3f * h \mid h \in H \}\) and \(r^2H = \{ r^2 * h \mid h \in H \}\). How do these compare to the cosets above?
