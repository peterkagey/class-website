  1. Prove that the subgroup \(\langle 8, 14\rangle \leq (\mathbb{Z},+)\) is a cyclic subgroup of the integers by describing an element \(n \in \mathbb{Z}\) such that \(\langle 8, 14\rangle = \langle n \rangle.\)
  2. This year, my birthday was on a Sunday. Next year is not a leap year. Find \(0 \leq k < 7\) such that \[ 365 \equiv k \pmod 7, \] and use this to determine what day of the week my birthday will be in 2027. What day of the week will my birthday fall on in 2028? (My birthday occurs after Leap Day in 2028.)
