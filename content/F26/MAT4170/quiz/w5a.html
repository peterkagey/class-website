Choose one of the following two parts to attempt.

  1. Let \(G = D_3\) and let \(H = \langle f \rangle\). Can you find an example of \(a, a', b, b' \in G\) such that \[ \begin{align*} aH &= a'H \\ bH &= b'H \\ (a*b)H &\neq (a'*b')H \end{align*} \]
  2. Prove that if \(G = D_4\) and \(N = \langle r^2 \rangle\), then if \(a' \in aN\) and \(b' \in bN\), then \({a' * b' \in (a * b)N}\).
    (Note: \(N\) is an example of a normal subgroup.)
