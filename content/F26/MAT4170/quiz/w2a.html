Recall that the dihedral group \(D_4 = \langle r, f \mid r^4 = f^2 = (rf)^2 = e \rangle\) describes the symmetries of a square. Draw a Cayley diagram of \(D_4\) using the generators \(r\) and \(f\).
(Hint: remember that we can label the corners of the square to keep track of the symmetries.)
Can the dihedral group \(D_4\) be generated with the symmetries \(f\) and \(rf\)? If so, draw a Cayley diagram using these generators. If not, why not?
