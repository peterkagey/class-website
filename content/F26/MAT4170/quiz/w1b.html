  1. Write \(\pi\) in word notation.
  2. Write \(\pi\) in cycle notation.
  3. Describe the identity permutation \(e\) in word notation, cycle notation, or in a sentence.
  4. Draw a diagram \(\sigma\), the permutation that satisfies the equation \(\sigma \circ \pi = e\). (Note that we call \(\sigma = \pi^{-1}\) the inverse of \(\pi\).)
