We denote the integer multiples of \(7\) as \[ 7\mathbb{Z} = \{7n \mid n \in \mathbb{Z}\} = \{\cdots, -14, -7, 0, 7, 14, \cdots\}. \] Prove or disprove that \((7\mathbb{Z}, +)\) forms a group, where \(+\) denotes ordinary addition.
(Make sure to say something about closure, associativity, identity, and inverses.)
