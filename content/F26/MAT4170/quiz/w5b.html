Choose one of the following two parts to attempt.

  1. Prove that in \(D_3\), the subgroup \(\langle r^2f \rangle\) is not a normal subgroup.
  2. Prove that in \(D_{2n}\), the dihedral group of the \(2n\)-gon, the subgroup \(\langle r^n \rangle\) is a normal subgroup.
    (Hint: recall that we can write every element in \(D_{2n}\) in the form \(r^k\) or \(r^kf\).)
