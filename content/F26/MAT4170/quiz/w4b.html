  1. Give an example of a group \(G\), a subgroup \(H \leq G\), and an element \(a \in G\) such that the coset \(aH\) is not a subgroup of \(G\).

  2. Let \((G,*)\) be a group, \(H \leq G\) be a subgroup, and \(a \in G\). Prove that the coset \[ aH = \{ a * h \mid h \in H \} \] is a subgroup of \(G\) if and only if \(a \in H\).
