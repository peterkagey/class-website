[10] Prove the second isomorphism theorem states that if \((G,*)\) is a group, $H \leq G$, $N \triangleleft G$, and \[ SN = \{s * n \mid s \in S \text{ and } n \in N\}, \] then the following quotient groups are isomorphic: \[ (SN)/N \cong S/(S \cap N). \] Before proving this, you should check that (a) \(SN \leq G\), (b) \(N \triangleleft SN\), and (c) \((S \cap N) \triangleleft S\).
  2. [10] Prove the third isomorphism theorem, which states that if \(N \triangleleft G\) and \(N \leq K \triangleleft G\), then \[ (G/N)/(K/N) \cong G/K. \] (You should also prove that \(N \triangleleft K\).)
