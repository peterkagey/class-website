1. Find all integers \(n \in \{0, 1, 2, \dots, 7\}\) with a multiplicative inverse modulo \(8\), meaning that there exists an integer \(k\) such that \(nk \equiv 1 \pmod 8\). Show that these form a group with the binary operation of "multiplication modulo \(8\)," which we denote \((\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z})^\times.\)
  2. Recall that a group \(G\) is called cyclic if it can be generated by a single element: \({G = \langle g \rangle.}\)
    1. Determine if \((\mathbb{Z}/7\mathbb{Z})^\times\) is a cyclic group and write down all of its subgroups.
    2. Give two examples of subgroups of \((\mathbb{Z}/15\mathbb{Z})^\times\) of order \(4\). Make sure one is a cyclic subgroup, and another is not cyclic.
    3. Determine if \((\mathbb{Z}/24 \mathbb{Z})^\times\) is a cyclic group, and find a subgroup of order \(4\).
