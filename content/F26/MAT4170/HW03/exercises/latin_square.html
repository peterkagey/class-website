Notice that the following $5 \times 5$ multiplication table has the Latin Square (sudoku) property, in that each row and column has no duplicate values. You will determine whether or not this is a Cayley table for a group.* \[ \begin{array}{c|ccccc} * & e & a & b & c & d \\ \hline e & e & a & b & c & d \\ a & a & e & c & d & b \\ b & b & d & e & a & c \\ c & c & b & d & e & a \\ d & d & c & a & b & e \end{array} \]

  1. Does this operation have an identity element? Explain why or why not.
  2. Does this operation have inverses? Explain why or why not.
  3. Is this binary operation associative? Prove why or why not.
