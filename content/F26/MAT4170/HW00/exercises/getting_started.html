  3. [2] Spend 10–20 minutes completing the following survey. You can either handwrite your responses and include scans in your PDF, or you can write your responses in \(\LaTeX\) by using the following snippet: 
    \begin{enumerate}
  \item[a.] As a person, ...
  \item[b.] As a student, ...
  \item[c.] Outside of school, ...
\end{enumerate}
    1. Tell me about yourself as a person.
      For example, you might answer questions such as: What's important to you? What's your family structure like? What's something you wish more people knew about you?
    2. Tell me about yourself as a student.
      For example: What's your major? How long have you been at CPP? What is an academic topic that you're curious about? How are you feeling about this class? Is there anything that CPP offers that you want to try out?
    3. What do you like to do outside of school?
      e.g., play tennis, crochet, read fashion magazines, watch anime, make crossword puzzles, play video games
    4. What's something you'd recommend that you don't think I've heard of?
      e.g., a YouTube channel, a restaurant, a musical artist, a garden, an iPhone app, a method for tying your shoelaces
    5. What would you like to do after college?
      e.g. get a job with NASA, move back to your hometown, run for political office, ride your bike up to Canada. (If you don't know yet, describe one possible future that you could see for yourself.)
    6. Tell me about your favorite teacher. What made them so good?
      This might be a high school English teacher, a religious leader, a tutor, a sports coach, or anyone else that you consider to be a teacher.
    7. Anything else you want me to know? [Optional]
      Are you a caretaker? Do you have a job that takes up a lot of your time? Do you have a long commute? Anything you're feeling worried about with this class? Are there ways that you need flexibility from me?
