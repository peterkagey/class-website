Course description

Introduction to the use of \(\LaTeX\) or other typesetting language for mathematics, with a particular emphasis on document preparation for instructional materials, articles, presentations, and Master's theses.

Expected Outcome

  1. Students will master the basics of \(\LaTeX\) to type mathematical symbols and equations.
  2. Students will use the \(\LaTeX\) numbering systems to create automatic numbering and references of chapters, sections, theorems, etc.
  3. Students will learn a variety of environments, including enumerated lists, itemized lists, the tabular environment, the align environment.
  4. Students will be ready to begin writing a thesis.
  5. Students will create a presentation using Beamer or other \(\LaTeX\) based presentation class.

Grading

The grade in this course is intended to reflect your learning, growth, and hard work.

Class policies

Academic Honesty and the Student Conduct Code

My goal for this class is for you to learn and grow, and your academic honesty is a crucial part of ensuring that happens. In this course, you are encouraged to ask for extensions when you need them, and you will be allowed to re-submit homework for full credit.

You are encouraged to collaborate with other students, come to drop-in hours, ask questions in class, and reach out to me via email when you need help with completing homework assignments or understanding course content. When submitting work done in collaboration with others, acknowledge their contributions, and write up the ideas in your own words.

In this course, you are permitted to use tools such as generative AI (e.g. Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT), Question and Answer sites (e.g. Math StackExchange), and other online resources (e.g. Google, GitHub) given that you acknowledge your use of these tools whenever they impact your written work, and that you share any insights you gained from using them with the class via the #hints-questions-tips channel on Discord.

If it appears to me that students are using these tools in a way that undermines their learning or engagement, I may ask individual students to stop using these tools.
